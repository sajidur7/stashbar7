FAQ

Questions & answers

Is Stashbar really free?

Yes, completely free. Download the DMG, drag Stashbar into Applications, and start stashing links immediately.

Do you read or upload my clipboard?

Stashbar only checks your clipboard locally on your Mac to spot web addresses, and ignores concealed items from password managers. Nothing is uploaded unless you stash a link while signed in.

What happens if I delete the app?

Guest links live only on your Mac (you can export a .json or .html backup anytime). If you sign in with Google or Apple, your links sync back automatically on any Mac.

Which Macs and macOS versions are supported?

macOS 13 Ventura or later, running natively as a universal binary on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

How do I quit Stashbar completely?

Right-click the pin icon in your menu bar and choose Quit Stashbar. It exits cleanly with no hidden helpers or background agents left running.
